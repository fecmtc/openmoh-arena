OpenMoH Arena - work by others in this build
============================================

The optional modern UI and the optional Omaha sound mixer come from
Project: Omaha by JayRewd, a fork of OpenMoHAA published under the GNU
General Public License, version 2 or later:
  https://github.com/JayRewd/omaha
OpenMoH Arena removed parts of it and limits what it may change.
OpenMoH Arena is not affiliated with or endorsed by Project: Omaha.

The modern UI
-------------

The modern UI is off unless the player turns it on in the launcher.

Code built into openmohaa.exe for the modern UI. The sources and the full
license texts are in code/thirdparty of the OpenMoH Arena source code:
  - TinyXML-2 10.0.0 by Lee Thomason (zlib license)
  - libtess2 by Mikko Mononen, from the SGI tessellator by Eric Veach.
    Copyright (C) Silicon Graphics, Inc. SGI Free Software License B,
    version 2.0: http://oss.sgi.com/projects/FreeB/
  - stb_truetype and stb_image_write by Sean Barrett (public domain)

Files in main/zz_moharena_ui.pk3:
  - fonts/Oswald-Medium.ttf and fonts/Oswald-SemiBold.ttf
    Copyright 2016 The Oswald Project Authors
    (https://github.com/googlefonts/OswaldFont). SIL Open Font License,
    version 1.1. The license is fonts/OFL.txt in the same pack.
  - ui/modern (menus, HUDs and their images) and sound/prom/hitmarkers:
    from Project: Omaha, as published in its repository.

The Omaha sound mixer
---------------------

The Omaha mixer is off unless the player picks it (s_backend omaha);
OpenAL stays the default.

Code built into openmohaa.exe for the Omaha mixer. The sources and the
full license texts are in code/thirdparty of the OpenMoH Arena source
code; the notices that must travel with the program are in
OMAHA-SOUND-LICENSES.txt next to this file:
  - libmysofa by Symonics GmbH and Christian Hoene (BSD 3-clause), with
    parts of the Speex resampler by Jean-Marc Valin and Thorvald Natvig
    (BSD 3-clause)
  - PFFFT by Julien Pommier, Hayati Ayguen and Dario Mambro, from
    FFTPACK by UCAR (FFTPACK license)
  - miniaudio by David Reid (Unlicense or MIT No Attribution)

Files in main/sound/prom:
  - hrtf/SADIE_KU100_DFC_256_order_fir_48000.sofa: the Neumann KU100
    head-related transfer functions from the SADIE database, Copyright
    University of York. Apache License 2.0. The license and the citation
    are hrtf/SADIE_LICENSE.txt and hrtf/README.txt in the same folder.
