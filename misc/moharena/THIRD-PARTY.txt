OpenMoH Arena - work by others in the optional modern UI
========================================================

The modern UI is off unless the player turns it on in the launcher. It
comes from Project: Omaha by JayRewd, a fork of OpenMoHAA published under
the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later:
  https://github.com/JayRewd/omaha
OpenMoH Arena removed parts of it and limits what it may change.
OpenMoH Arena is not affiliated with or endorsed by Project: Omaha.

Code built into openmohaa.exe for the modern UI. The sources and the full
license texts are in code/thirdparty of the OpenMoH Arena source code:
  - TinyXML-2 10.0.0 by Lee Thomason (zlib license)
  - libtess2 by Mikko Mononen, from the SGI tessellator by Eric Veach.
    Copyright (C) Silicon Graphics, Inc. SGI Free Software License B,
    version 2.0: http://oss.sgi.com/projects/FreeB/
  - stb_truetype and stb_image_write by Sean Barrett (public domain)
