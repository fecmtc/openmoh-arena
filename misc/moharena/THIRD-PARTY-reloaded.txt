OpenMoH Arena - work by others in this build
============================================

pstngh, with testing and ideas by notuern, a fork of OpenMoHAA published
under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later:
  https://github.com/fortemoh/MoHAA-Reloaded (branch vulkan)
OpenMoH Arena is not affiliated with or endorsed by MoHAA-Reloaded.

The Omaha sound mixer (s_backend omaha) comes from Project: Omaha by
JayRewd, a fork of OpenMoHAA published under the GNU General Public
License, version 2 or later:
  https://github.com/JayRewd/omaha
OpenMoH Arena is not affiliated with or endorsed by Project: Omaha.

Code built into the game for the Omaha mixer. The sources and the full
license texts are in code/thirdparty of the OpenMoH Arena source code;
the notices that must travel with the program are in

    FFTPACK by UCAR (FFTPACK license)

Files in main/sound/prom:
  - hrtf/SADIE_KU100_DFC_256_order_fir_48000.sofa: the Neumann KU100
    head-related transfer functions from the SADIE database, Copyright
    University of York. Apache License 2.0. The license and the citation
    are hrtf/SADIE_LICENSE.txt and hrtf/README.txt in the same folder.
  - hitmarkers/beep.wav and hitmarkers/classic.wav: from Project: Omaha,
    as published in its repository.

The licenses of the other libraries in this package are in the licenses
